# Proposed rulings — run 2026-10-21_doomdebates

### CL-86: Counted-elite (E-class) headcount for channels without composition data [Proposed 2026-10-21, by Claude]
**Rule:** When an applicant gives no audience-composition data, set the PERCYs E-class headcount (lab and government AI staff, AI-beat journalists) by scaling the nearest reference channel's ratio of elite monthly followers to unique viewers to the applicant's unique viewers, derived from watch-hours or views rather than subscriber counts. Adjust once for brand (for example a combative or partisan show versus a staff-recommended explainer) by at most ×0.5 or ×2. Put any further format objection in Δ_E or a CL-11 branch, never in the headcount again. Report the mean at half and at double the headcount.
**Why:** E rows are usually the largest rows in audio and video channel grants. Without a shared method, estimators set headcounts differing 2–4× and priced the same brand objection three times: in N, in Δ_E and in a values branch.
**Example (hypothetical):** A reference channel has 2,000 elite followers per 500,000 uniques. An applicant with 100,000 uniques starts at 400 elite followers, or 200 after a ×0.5 brand adjustment. Report the mean at both 100 and 400.

### CL-87: Marginal factor when an unpaid leader continues in both worlds [Proposed 2026-10-21, by Claude]
**Rule:** Apply this when an organisation's leader works full-time but unpaid, is not financially dependent on the grant, and states the output continues at reduced volume without it.
- Model the no-grant world as CL-23 downsizing states, not CL-70's part-time baseline and not CL-31 closure.
- Set the marginal factor as the share of the organisation-year that the grant restores.
- Do not stack a further CL-37 non-founder discount on top, unless the grant adds staff beyond those already in place.
- Report the no-grant output share, and report the organisation's average dollar both with and without the leader's labour imputed (CL-54).
**Why:** Estimators who agreed on the no-grant output share still differed by a quarter or more on whether to apply the CL-37 discount a second time. No ruling covers a leader who is full-time but unpaid.
