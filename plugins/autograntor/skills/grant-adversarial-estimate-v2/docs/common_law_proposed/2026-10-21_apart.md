# Proposed rulings — run 2026-10-21_apart

### CL-89: Career entries caused directly by mass-participation events [Proposed 2026-10-21, by Claude]
**Rule:** When a grant funds hackathons, sprints or similar mass-participation events, price the career entries they cause directly (not through a downstream fellowship or programme already priced in the same ledger) as a per-event rate against the Proposed "junior researcher enters a sustained AI-safety research role" milestone. Anchor the rate on the best documented local figure for in-person events with organiser follow-up, and scale it down (≈×0.25–0.5) for remote or multi-hub formats and for the absence of a follow-up pipeline. Count only entries that route through no other row (CL-5), and state the no-grant event count so the rate applies to the extra events only.
**Why:** CL-84 covers short mentorships, not open events, and estimators priced the same marginal event at rates differing about threefold by choosing between an in-person local anchor and a single documented hire; in a talent-heavy grant that choice moved the headline by more than a tenth.
**Example (hypothetical):** A local in-person series is documented at 0.4 entries per event. Five extra remote, multi-hub events at ×0.3 give 0.6 expected entries, each priced at the milestone weight.
