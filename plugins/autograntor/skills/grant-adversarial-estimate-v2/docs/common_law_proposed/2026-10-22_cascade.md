# Proposed rulings — run 2026-10-22_cascade

### CL-89: Opportunity cost of founders' time at a new organisation [Proposed 2026-10-22, by Claude]
**Rule:** When a grant pays the founders of a new organisation whose no-grant world is CL-70's part-time baseline, charge their displaced time under CL-4 at the value of the output they would otherwise have produced, priced on the same scale as the ledger's own rows (PERCYs rates for a communicator, the researcher-year only for a person in a funded research role, a stated adjacent fraction otherwise), multiplied by the probability that the alternative work happens at all and by the share the grant displaces. Do not use the generic researcher-year price by default, and do not apply CL-67's price without its scope condition (a role at another safety organisation that will be refilled). Enter the quantity once: either as the CL-70 baseline netted against the grant's increment or as a displacement charge, never both, and never at a per-person rate above the rate at which the same ledger values the funded work itself.
**Why:** Without a rule, estimators priced the same founders' displaced time at zero and at a marginal-researcher rate, a gap large enough on its own to decide the sign of a small grant. The charge is the founders' no-grant output entering from the other side of the ledger, so it must be priced on that output's own scale.
**Example (hypothetical):** A founder who would otherwise run a small, unproven channel worth about 8 per year, with a 70% chance of doing so, and who is displaced 85% for one quarter, is charged about 0.25 × 8 × 0.7 × 0.85 ≈ 1.2, not 0.25 × 50 ≈ 12.5.
