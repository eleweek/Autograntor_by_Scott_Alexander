# Proposed rulings, run 2026-11-12 (label: paradigm3)

Written by the coordinator from the judge's candidates. Highest number before this file: CL-92 (2026-11-11_goodancestors-nz.md). Both rulings passed the README bar: no existing ruling covers the situation (CL-91 is silent on the rate; CL-84 and CL-89 in 2026-10-21_apart.md cover mentorships and events, not salaried hires), each moved a headline mean by more than about 10%, and each is a situation other grants will raise.

### CL-93: Which dollars are lost when outside money is displaced [Proposed 2026-11-12, by Claude (judge)]
**Rule:** Under CL-91(c), price the programme value lost on displaced dollars as a stated blend of two rates: the least valuable dollars the organisation spends in the with-grant world on lines it can trim without dismissing anyone (compute, unfilled roles, contractors), and the top of its general-support stack (runway). State the weight on each; default about two-thirds on the trimmable lines when such lines exist at that grant size, and all on the runway rate when they do not. Never price the loss at the rate of a tranche the grant does not include, and report the mean under each pure rate.
**Why:** CL-91 says to subtract the value lost but not at what rate. Estimators chose the next tranche above the grant and the weakest tranche inside it, which differed by half and could make a positive tranche look negative.
**Example (hypothetical):** A grant displaces $200K. Its weakest trimmable line is worth 40 per $1M and runway 120 per $1M. At two-thirds and one-third the loss is 0.2 × (0.67 × 40 + 0.33 × 120) ≈ 13, against 8 and 24 under the pure rates.

### CL-94: Career-entry credit for full-time hires from outside the field [Proposed 2026-11-12, by Claude (judge)]
**Rule:** When a grant pays a full-time research hire at an existing organisation, the hire's funded year belongs to the output rows and the following year to CL-38. A separate talent row may credit only the years after that, as P(hire comes from outside safety work) × P(would not have entered anyway) × P(stays in the field) × the remaining career years priced as in the Proposed junior-researcher milestone, with a stated factor for roles that are not alignment research. The shares of hires from outside the field and from other safety organisations (CL-67) must sum to at most one.
**Why:** CL-84 and CL-89 (2026-10-21_apart.md) cover mentorships and events, not salaried hires. Without a rule, the same hire was priced at zero and at a value that counted the funded and following years a second time.
**Example (hypothetical):** A hire has a 0.3 chance of coming from outside the field, 0.5 of not entering anyway, 0.6 of staying, and later years worth 60: the row is 0.3 × 0.5 × 0.6 × 60 ≈ 5.
