# Proposed rulings — run 2026-10-22_doomdebates-rerun

### CL-90: CL-26 rate for ongoing channels, and per-entrant price by role [Proposed 2026-10-22, by Claude]
**Rule:** CL-26's anchor is a lifetime rate per person reached, so for an ongoing channel apply it to the people who newly join the returning core in the funded year (plus a small drip, roughly a tenth of the rate, from the continuing core and drive-by tail), never to the whole core each year. Keep the explicitness factor at or near 1 for a work that names the field, and put "would have entered anyway" (an already-sympathetic audience, a crowded field) in a separate stated factor. Price each entrant by the role mix the channel plausibly produces: research entrants at the Proposed junior-researcher milestone, advocacy or communications entrants at a stated lower price built from advocate-years.
**Why:** Applied per viewer-year, the anchor produces a fresh cohort every year from the same loyal audience and can triple a media grant's talent row; applying the explicitness cut to a work that names the field prices the same consideration twice.
**Example (hypothetical):** A channel with a 20K returning core that gains 8K new core viewers a year, naming the field explicitly, with a 0.4 already-sympathetic factor: 8K × 1/5K × 0.4 + drip ≈ 0.9 entrants a year, at a 0.2 research / 0.8 advocacy blend ≈ 70 each.
